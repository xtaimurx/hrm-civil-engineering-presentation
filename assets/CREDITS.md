# Image credits

All photographs are from Wikimedia Commons. Images are resized to a maximum of
1600 px on the long edge and re-encoded for the web; framing and cropping are
applied in CSS at runtime.

## Facts

The Empire State Building figures quoted in the deck (about 3,400 workers at
peak, 410 days to build, 102 floors, five official deaths) are widely cited but
should be checked against a primary source before presenting.
